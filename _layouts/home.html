The product

One product, done properly

We do one thing: premium pine shavings for animal bedding. Every bale is kiln-dried, screened and dust-extracted before it's packaged into 14kg bales.

Kiln-dried for absorbency

Shavings are kiln-dried to a moisture content of {{ site.moisture }} — dry enough to soak up what your animals leave behind, which keeps stables and sheds drier for longer between changes.

Screened & dust-extracted

All of our shavings pass through a purpose-built three-part screen. The first 'star screen' removes any oversize pieces, with the remaining shavings entering a rotary trommel screen that extracts the sawdust and fines. The final phase of the screening process removes the smaller airborne dust particles.

100% natural pine

No chemical additives, just radiata pine shavings from kiln-dried timber. Suited for horses, poultry and small animals (from rats and rabbits to reptiles).

Handled once

Our Tumut plant sits in the heart of NSW softwood country. Shavings go from the mill into our baler with minimal handling.

How it's made

From the saw to the shed

  1. 01

    Sawmill

    Shavings are a by-product of milling kiln-dried radiata pine — timber that's already been dried for structural use.

  2. 02

    Screened and dust extracted

    A rotary screen separates out sawdust and fines, leaving only the coarse, absorbent shavings curl. The airborne dust is extracted before baling.

  3. 03

    Bale press

    Pressed into {{ site.bale_weight }} bales that expand to {{ site.bale_volume }}. Dense to freight, easy to store.

Since 2002

Holbrook to Tumut

Superior Shavings started baling in Holbrook, NSW in 2002. In 2019 we moved the plant to Tumut, in the foothills of the Snowy Mountains and one of the biggest softwood milling regions in the country.

Today we're part of the Breeder's Choice Woodshavings family, alongside our sister plant in Colac, Victoria. Two plants, two states, the same standard: kiln-dried, screened, dust-extracted pine — and nothing else.
