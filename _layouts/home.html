The product

One product, done properly

We do one thing: premium pine shavings for animal bedding. Every bale is kiln-dried, screened and dust-extracted before it's pressed.

Kiln-dried for absorbency

Shavings are kiln-dried to a moisture content of {{ site.moisture }} — dry enough to soak up what your animals leave behind, which keeps stables and sheds drier for longer between changes.

Screened & dust-extracted

Fine dust and sawdust are screened out before baling — less of the airborne dust that aggravates airways in horses with respiratory sensitivities, and a cleaner shed for poultry too.

100% natural pine

No chemical additives — just radiata pine shavings from kiln-dried timber. Suited to horses, poultry, and small animals from rats and rabbits to reptiles.

Handled once

Our Tumut plant sits in the heart of NSW softwood country. Shavings go from the mill into our baler with minimal handling — fewer steps between the saw and your shed.

How it's made

From the saw to the shed

  1. 01

    Sawmill

    Shavings are a by-product of milling kiln-dried radiata pine — timber that's already been dried for structural use.

  2. 02

    Screen

    A rotary screen separates out sawdust and fines, leaving only the coarse, absorbent curl.

  3. 03

    Dust extraction

    Airborne dust is extracted before baling — what's left is the bedding, not the by-product of the by-product.

  4. 04

    Bale press

    Pressed into {{ site.bale_weight }} poly-wrapped bales that expand to {{ site.bale_volume }} — dense to freight, easy to store.

Since 2002

Holbrook to Tumut

Superior Shavings started baling at the Mill Industrial Estate in Holbrook, NSW in 2002. In 2019 we moved the plant to Tumut, in the foothills of the Snowy Mountains — one of the biggest softwood milling regions in the country — to be closer to the timber.

Today we're part of the Breeder's Choice Woodshavings family, alongside our sister plant in Colac, Victoria. Two plants, two states, the same standard: kiln-dried, screened, dust-extracted pine — and nothing else.
